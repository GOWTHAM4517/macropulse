About MacroPulse

One dashboard, every major market

MacroPulse is an economic intelligence dashboard built to track the markets people actually ask about: precious metals, the rupee, the world's two biggest cryptocurrencies, and India's benchmark index — each with its own live chart. Beneath it all sits seven decades of World Bank GDP data, rendered as an animated race between the world's largest economies. Pick a card below to open that market's own page.
